Sources and coverage

Robinhood

Current positions, quantities, cost basis, account cash, and recorded trades and distributions come from the signed-in Robinhood account.

Yahoo Finance

Provides public closing prices, fundamentals, fund holdings, analyst estimates, and news. Valuation coverage counts the value of direct stocks with a positive trailing P/E.

Portfolio history and VOO

History reconstructs securities held on each day from recorded trades and market closes. The latest portfolio value uses Robinhood’s current marks. VOO replays the same dated investment cash flows, including dividend reinvestments.

Local storage

Portfolio data is served to this browser through the local app. Saved watchlists, targets, and alerts stay in this browser. Public market requests use ticker symbols.
